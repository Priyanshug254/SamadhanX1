## 🎬 Detailed Step-by-Step Presentation Script

### Step 1: Citizen Societal Challenge Ingestion (0:00 - 1:30)
- **Screen**: Mobile Citizen App (or Web Citizen Portal).
- **Presenter Action**:
  1. Demonstrate the challenge submission:
     - **Title**: *Severe Fluoride & Arsenic Contamination in Rural Hand Pumps*
     - **District**: *Chandauli, Uttar Pradesh*
     - **Location Coordinates**: `25.2612° N, 83.2644° E`
     - **Affected Population**: `2,400 villagers`
  2. Click **Submit**.
  3. Show the instant tracking code generated: `SMX-2026-08-00101`.
- **Presenter Dialogue**:
  > *"Judges, SamadhanX bridges the critical gap between grassroots civic problems and national research institutions. When a citizen in Chandauli encounters toxic groundwater, they don't just file an anonymous complaint—they provide geotagged photographic evidence directly into our national pipeline."*

### Step 2: Real AI Intelligence & Prioritization (1:30 - 3:00)
- **Screen**: Government Command Center (`http://localhost:5173/government`).
- **Presenter Action**:
  1. Open Challenge Dossier `SMX-2026-08-00101`.
  2. Highlight the **AI Diagnostics Card**:
     - **Predicted Domain**: *Water & Sanitation (96% confidence)*
     - **Priority Score**: *94.5 / 100* (Severity: CRITICAL, Urgency: IMMEDIATE)
     - **Explainability**: Shows why priority was boosted (+25 due to toxic non-degradable chemical health hazard).
     - **Duplicate Detection**: Verifies nearest comparable issue is 18.4 km away with a different chemical profile.
     - **Model Attribution**: Google Gemini 1.5-Flash (with deterministic fail-safe).
- **Presenter Dialogue**:
  > *"Notice how the AI doesn't just categorize—it reasons. It detects that standard bleaching powder cartridges saturate in 3 weeks and flags this as an intractable challenge requiring academic R&D rather than routine municipal patching."*
